# E1 — Filter isolation (measured)

First real slice of experiment E1 (`docs/experiments.md`). Config-diff: **only the Hessian filter varies**; energy (symmetric Dirichlet), mesh, initialization, line search (Armijo), linear solver (dense), and criterion (`|g|inf < 1e-6`) are held fixed. Numbers are produced by `python -m bench.run_e1` (reproducible; seed fixed) and gated on `bench/conformance.py`.

Scenario: 10x10 grid, boundary pinned at rest, interior perturbed (amp=0.0280, seed=7); init energy 14.1771; 162 free dofs.

| filter | status | iters | final energy | wall (ms) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| none | nondescent | 15 | 4.051084 | 1946.9 |
| clamp | converged | 10 | 4.000000 | 1157.6 |
| absolute | converged | 15 | 4.000000 | 1800.7 |
| project-on-demand | converged | 10 | 4.000000 | 1271.6 |
| identity-shift | converged | 15 | 4.000000 | 2100.1 |
| global-pdn | converged | 15 | 4.000000 | 2021.2 |

All converged solvers reached the same minimum energy (4.000000) — confirming the filter changes the *path* (iterations/robustness), not the solution. `none` (full Newton, unfiltered) is expected to stall on indefinite Hessians; the filtered variants are the point of the comparison.

_Caveat: this is a small controlled prototype (dense solve, one scenario/seed) to validate the harness and produce the first measured numbers — not yet the full E1 (no ν-sweep, no locking-free element C1, no official-code port). See `docs/experiments.md`._
