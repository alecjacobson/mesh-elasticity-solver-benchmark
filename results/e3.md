# E3 — BCQN triple-split: the barrier-aware line-search main effect (measured)

Isolates ONE of BCQN's three factors — the **line search** — holding direction (L-BFGS) and criterion (‖g‖∞) fixed, swapping only `{backtracking → barrier-aware}` (the CCD-style inversion-free step cap, `bench/barrier_ls.py`, conformance-gated). 5 seeds × 2 strata on symmetric Dirichlet. Run: `python -m bench.run_e3`.

| stratum | line search | converged | iters (median) | energy-evals (median) | energy-evals (max) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| typical | backtracking | 5/5 | 51 | 61 | 74 |
| typical | barrier-aware | 5/5 | 52 | 54 | 58 |
| adversarial (near-inversion) | backtracking | 5/5 | 54 | 67 | 69 |
| adversarial (near-inversion) | barrier-aware | 5/5 | 54 | 56 | 61 |

## Observed (line-search main effect — an honest qualification of BCQN Fig.6)

- **Energy-eval cost (the backtracking cost) drops, modestly.** Adversarial stratum: median energy-evals **67→56** (max 69→61); typical: 61→54. Backtracking from α=1 overshoots into the energy's +∞ region and must halve its way back; the barrier cap never enters it. The effect is **regime-specific** (bigger near inversion) but **single-digit-percent, not the >10× BCQN Fig.6 implies**.
- **No robustness difference on this energy.** Success is identical (**5/5 both** adversarial; 5/5 both typical) and iterations barely move (54↔54). The reason is a genuine **confound**: symmetric Dirichlet is *itself* a barrier energy (+∞ at J≤0), so plain backtracking already cannot step through an inversion — the barrier-aware line search is **partly redundant with the energy's own barrier**. It saves wasted backtracks; it does not rescue a solve that would otherwise fail, because none fail.
- **What this means for the claim.** BCQN's dramatic line-search win is expected to appear on **non-barrier** energies or **un-guarded** initializations (where a bad step genuinely inverts and kills the solve); on a barrier distortion energy with an inversion-free init, the same component is only an efficiency tweak. So `bcqn→{l-bfgs,aqp}` stays **qualified**: the line-search factor helps, but its magnitude is energy/regime dependent and here it is minor — not a stand-alone >10× robustness multiplier.
- **Attribution (partial factorial).** This isolates the line-search factor only; the direction factor (blended-Sobolev proxy) is in `results/world1_profiles.md` (helps only ill-conditioned), and the characteristic-gradient criterion is a criterion swap (deferred). Together they compose the 2³; this is the main-effect slice for the cheapest factor.

_Caveat: symmetric Dirichlet, 2D, single mesh size; energy-evals is the HW-independent backtracking-cost proxy (docs/metrics.md). Full 2³ factorial (× blended-Sobolev direction × characteristic-gradient criterion) is the remaining E3 work; this is the main-effect slice._
